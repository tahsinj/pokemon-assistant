# Team model against Classic: National Dex OU

Measured by `npm run teamproof` on 2026-10-15. A box of 30 Pokémon drawn from
National Dex OU usage (about a third with an off-meta change: an odd spread, a random
move or no item). Classic and the team model each picked a team from it; each
team then played 500 games against 100 high-rated meta teams from
2026-10, the same opponent and seed for both, with the Search bot on both
sides.

| Team | Members | Meta score | Win rate (95% interval) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Team model | Zamazenta, Latios-Mega, Victini, Meowscarada, Blacephalon, Tapu Lele | 58.4% | 56.8% (52.4% to 61.1%) |
| Classic | Landorus-Therian, Latios-Mega, Kingambit, Garchomp, Zamazenta, Kyurem | 52.8% | 69.4% (65.2% to 73.3%) |

Difference, team model minus Classic, game by game: -12.6% (-18.5% to -6.7%).
