# Team model against Classic: Gen 9 OU

Measured by `npm run teamproof` on 2026-10-14. A box of 30 Pokémon drawn from
Gen 9 OU usage (about a third with an off-meta change: an odd spread, a random
move or no item). Classic and the team model each picked a team from it; each
team then played 500 games against 100 high-rated meta teams from
2026-10, the same opponent and seed for both, with the Search bot on both
sides.

| Team | Members | Meta score | Win rate (95% interval) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Team model | Kingambit, Dragapult, Iron Boulder, Weavile, Hydreigon, Iron Valiant | 56.7% | 50.0% (45.6% to 54.4%) |
| Classic | Gholdengo, Kyurem, Cinderace, Great Tusk, Iron Valiant, Ogerpon-Wellspring | 49.5% | 48.6% (44.2% to 53.0%) |

Difference, team model minus Classic, game by game: +1.4% (-4.3% to 7.1%).
